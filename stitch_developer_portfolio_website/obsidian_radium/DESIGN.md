## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a high-contrast radioactive cyber-terminal aesthetic, fusing avant-garde display geometry with raw instrumentation telemetry. It is engineered for advanced technical interfaces, algorithmic execution platforms, and tactical developer environments that demand intense focus, visceral power, and surgical precision.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Tactical Cyber-Brutalism & Instrument Glass:** Pure obsidian slate foundations overlaid with calibrated micro-borders, razor-sharp HUD indicators, and radioactive luminescence.
- **Volatile Kinetic Accents:** High-potency radium illumination (`#39FF14`) treated as targeted optical energy rather than generic decoration. Light cuts through atmospheric dark space with precision focus.
- **Controlled Telemetry:** Raw monospace data metrics balance the experimental, high-fashion display geometries of Syne. Every screen acts as a responsive digital instrumentation deck.

### Emotional Demeanor
- **Hyper-Focused & Lethal:** Unapologetic density, absolute legibility, zero corporate softness.
- **Avant-Garde Precision:** High aesthetic tension between experimental structural titles and mission-critical telemetry readouts.

## Colors

The palette operates under a high-dynamic-range dark environment. Surfaces are constructed from deep obsidian layers tinted with subtle ultraviolet undertones, providing maximum contrast for radioactive primary emissions.

### Primary Spectrum (`#39FF14` — Radium Green)
The core kinetic anchor. Reserved for primary operational actions, active terminal cursors, high-priority telemetry signals, and radioactive focus rings. Emits an electric green frequency that commands instant gaze fixation.

### Secondary Spectrum (`#00FF88` — Cyber Emerald)
A stabilization tone. Applied to confirmed operational statuses, nominal telemetric feeds, data visualization streams, and secondary interactive components. Softens the extreme frequency of pure radium.

### Tertiary Spectrum (`#7B2CBF` — Deep Ultraviolet)
A grounding spectral counterweight. Used for atmospheric background depth, subtle linear dividing rules, passive card outlines, and ambient illumination glow channels beneath high-intensity surfaces.

### Neutral Foundation (`#0A0A12` — Obsidian Slate)
A layered obsidian hierarchy engineered to eliminate display wash:
- `surface-base`: `#050508` — Void black backdrop.
- `surface-elevated`: `#0A0A12` — Root viewport layer.
- `surface-container`: `#12131F` — Component cards, dialogs, and terminal segments.
- `surface-border`: `#1F2238` — Low-contrast structural wireframes.
- `text-primary`: `#F0FFF0` — Radioactive phosphor white.
- `text-secondary`: `#8A94A6` — Desaturated steel slate.
- `text-muted`: `#495368` — Deep operational telemetry inactive tone.

## Typography

The typography creates a direct structural dialogue between the wide-stance avant-garde forms of **Syne** and the mechanical precision of **Space Mono**.

### Typographic Hierarchy Rules
- **Display & Headlines (`Syne`):** Use for high-impact structural focal points, section markers, and hero anchors. Syne brings sculptural, kinetic personality that sets the brand apart from generic dark dashboards.
- **Body & Data Telemetry (`Space Mono`):** All running text, interface controls, metric callouts, and system logs rely on Space Mono. Tabular numbers are default, preserving clean vertical scanning.
- **Case Formatting:**
  - `Syne` headlines: Mixed case with tight tracking to accentuate geometric letterforms.
  - Labels & Telemetry: Uppercase by default with loose tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.08em` to `0.12em`) for immediate military-grade legibility under rapid glance conditions.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a calibrated 12-column terminal grid on desktop, scaling to 6 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile screens. Structural spacing adheres to strict 8px/4px quantum intervals, ensuring rhythmic alignment across high-density operational panels.

### Form Factors & Adaptation
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns, `margin: 2.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Maximum content bounds stretch to 1600px for multi-stream data feeds, dashboards, and split telemetry panels.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 6 columns, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Side navigation docks collapse into vertical micro-symbol ribbons.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, `margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 0.75rem`. Complex telemetry ribbons stack into sequential vertical cards with sticky terminal controls.

### Density & Rhythms
- **High-Density Modules:** Use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for telemetry stat chips, code headers, and status flags.
- **Structural Containers:** Use `space-md` (16px) for interior card padding, and `space-lg` (24px) to separate independent subsystem viewports.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on diffused drop shadows. Depth is achieved via **tonal stratification**, **low-contrast micro-borders**, and **focused radium luminescence**.

### Depth Layers
- **Floor (Void):** `#050508` — Foundational background. Completely flat with an optional 3% noise texture or 48px micro-grid crosshairs.
- **Deck (Base):** `#0A0A12` — Standard viewport canvas, resting 1 tier above void.
- **Station (Surfaces):** `#12131F` with a 1px border of `rgba(57, 255, 20, 0.12)` or `#1F2238`. The default surface container.
- **Overdrive (Floating Drawers / Overlays):** `#161826` with a 1px border of `rgba(57, 255, 20, 0.35)` and an ambient optical aura: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(57, 255, 20, 0.15)`.

### Photonic Emission & Glow
- Static elements never use blurred dropshadows.
- Active states (focused inputs, engaged switches, critical alert states) project a sharp radioactive phosphor halo using localized dual shadows: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #39FF14, 0 0 16px rgba(57, 255, 20, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

The design system implements a controlled **Level 2 (Rounded)** shape model. While the atmosphere is strictly tactical, subtle curvature softens harsh corners and focuses the eye on content.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Standard UI Elements (`rounded`):** `0.5rem` (8px) for buttons, inputs, segmented controls, chips, and telemetry badges.
- **Structural Cards (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (16px) for panels, containers, modals, and preview panes.
- **Prominent Viewports (`rounded-xl`):** `1.5rem` (24px) for hero containers and overlay terminal hubs.
- **Status Indicators & Microdots:** Fully circular (`rounded-full`) for signal pulse beads, radar pings, and avatars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Radium Button:** Solid `#39FF14` background with pure obsidian text (`#050508`), font: `Space Mono`, weight: 700, uppercase. Hover shifts to `#00FF88` with a subtle kinetic glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(57, 255, 20, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary Ghost Button:** Translucent `#12131F` background, 1px border of `rgba(57, 255, 20, 0.3)`, text in `#39FF14`. Hover: border brightens to 100% `#39FF14` with `rgba(57, 255, 20, 0.08)` surface fill.
- **Destructive Button:** High-intensity crimson background (`#FF1447`) with obsidian text or border outline with soft crimson glow.

### Chips & Telemetry Badges
- Compact height (24px), font: `label-telemetry` (Space Mono 10px uppercase).
- Dark obsidian base (`#0A0A12`), 1px outline of secondary or primary color at 30% alpha.
- Left-aligned 6px radioactive micro-orb indicating status: green for nominal, amber for warning, violet for internal standby.

### Input Fields
- Background: `#080910`. Border: 1px solid `#1F2238`. Text: `#F0FFF0` (Space Mono 14px).
- Placeholder: `#495368`.
- Focus state: Border instantly snaps to `#39FF14` with a tight 3px photonic perimeter (`box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(57, 255, 20, 0.25)`).
- Optional inline trailing tag: Monospace command hotkey (e.g., `[CMD+K]`) rendered in `#495368`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 18x18px frame with `0.25rem` radius for checkboxes; circular for radios.
- Unchecked: `#0A0A12` base with 1px border `#2A2E46`.
- Checked: `#39FF14` fill with `#050508` check glyph or inner dot. Radiates a faint `rgba(57, 255, 20, 0.3)` glow.

### Cards & Telemetry Pods
- Background: `#12131F`. 
- Border: 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` with subtle corner bracket markers or top-edge accent line in `#39FF14` (2px height).
- Padding: `1.5rem`.
- Internal dividers: 1px dotted `#1F2238`.

### Telemetry Streamers & Lists
- Row items separated by hairline rules `#1F2238`.
- Alternating hover states introduce `rgba(57, 255, 20, 0.03)` highlight with a 2px left border accent in `#39FF14`.
- All quantitative fields right-aligned with fixed tabular monospace sizing.
